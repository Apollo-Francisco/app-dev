## About the Movie
**Evangelion: 3.0+1.0 Thrice Upon a Time** is the fourth and final film in the *Rebuild of Evangelion* series. It wraps up the story of **Shinji Ikari** and the battle against the Angels, and it brings the whole franchise to a close.

> "Sayonara, all of the Evangelions."

## Movie Details

|--------|------|

| Release Date | March 8, 2021 (Japan) |
| Runtime | About 155 minutes |
| Genre | Animation, Mecha, Sci-Fi, Drama |
| Theme Song | "One Last Kiss" by Utada Hikaru |

## Main Characters
- **Shinji Ikari** - the pilot of Evangelion Unit-01
- **Rei Ayanami** - pilot of Unit-00
- **Asuka Langley Shikinami** - pilot of Unit-02
- **Mari Illustrious Makinami** - pilot of Unit-05 and Unit-08
- **Misato Katsuragi** - leader of the WILLE crew
- **Gendo Ikari** - Shinji's father and head of the Human Instrumentality Project

## Why I Love It
1. The animation and mecha battles are stunning.
2. The soundtrack fits every emotional moment.
3. Shinji's growth makes the ending feel earned.
4. It gives the whole series a satisfying conclusion.
